01 What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let a site remember information about your visit — like preferences and session state — and are widely used to make sites work, or work better, as well as to report usage and serve relevant ads.

02 How we use cookies

Cloud iNIT uses cookies and similar technologies (like the Cloudflare edge beacon) to keep the site and sandbox functional, understand how the boot sequence and documentation are used, and — where you've consented — show relevant ads through Google AdSense, which helps keep the platform free.

03 Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

Required for core functionality — session state, security tokens, and remembering your cookie consent choice. These can't be switched off without breaking the site.

Performance & analytics

Help us understand aggregate usage — which pages are visited, how the status page performs, and where errors occur — so we can improve the boot sequence and documentation.

Advertising

Set by Google AdSense to show ads and, where permitted, personalize them based on your browsing. These only load with your consent where consent is legally required.

05 Third-party cookies

Google AdSense and the Cloudflare Insights beacon may set their own cookies when you visit the site. We don't control these cookies directly — they're governed by Google's Cookie Policy and Cloudflare's own privacy practices respectively.

06 Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block cookies entirely, block third-party cookies only, or clear existing cookies. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may break sandbox provisioning or login.

  • To opt out of personalized Google ads, visit adssettings.google.com.
  • The Digital Advertising Alliance offers a broader opt-out at optout.aboutads.info.
  • Most browsers also support "Do Not Track" and native cookie-blocking controls in their privacy settings.
